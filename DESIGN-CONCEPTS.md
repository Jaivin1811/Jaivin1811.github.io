# Design concepts for Jaivin's portfolio

## Concept A — "The Value Stream" (production-line metaphor)
Each section is a station on a horizontal production line. Scrolling moves a part along a conveyor, and each station "adds value" (About → Experience → Projects...). Kanban-style cards, takt-time progress bar.
- Strength: instantly says "industrial engineer" and Lean.
- Weakness: horizontal scroll-jacking is fragile on mobile and hurts accessibility and SEO. The metaphor is also common among Lean/ops portfolios.

## Concept B — "The Control Chart" (SPC and data-first)
The whole site is framed as a process under statistical control. The hero is a live control chart with UCL/LCL lines. Projects are plotted as points, skills as a Pareto chart, and the career journey is a run chart.
- Strength: strong link to quality, Minitab, OEE and Power BI work.
- Weakness: it leans on one side of the profile. Procurement, supply chain and CAD have no natural home, and a chart-heavy hero can read as cold.

## Concept C — "The Drawing Sheet" (metrology and engineering drawing)
The site is an engineering drawing sheet: a title block, datum markers, dimension lines with tolerances, section views and revision tables. The hero shows a precision-turned workpiece and a touch probe that follows the cursor and reports a live measurement. Skills appear as a Pareto chart, and the dark theme is a blueprint.
- Strength: it directly expresses the master's thesis topic (workpiece probe accuracy), GD&T/FAI inspection experience, NX/Teamcenter exposure and quality focus, while staying neutral enough to hold procurement and analytics content. Interactive without scroll-jacking, accessible, light on assets, and unlike a typical portfolio.
- Weakness: needs restraint so it stays elegant rather than gimmicky.

## Recommendation: Concept C, with one borrowed idea from B
Concept C is the strongest because it turns the thesis into the signature interaction, which is the thing a recruiter will remember ("the portfolio with the measuring probe"). I borrow Concept B's Pareto chart for the skills section so the data and quality side of the profile still shows.
